PARTNER LOGOS
=============
Put each partner's official logo here, named exactly as below (SVG preferred, PNG also works):

  microsoft.svg   google.svg   amazon.svg   deloitte.svg   pwc.svg
  johnson-and-johnson.svg   sap.svg   workday.svg   oracle.svg   servicenow.svg

- Use the official files from each company's brand / press page, or the files they send you.
- Transparent background. The site shows them in white automatically.
- Only display logos of organisations you have a confirmed partnership with and permission from.
- To add or remove partners, edit "logos" in src/data/academy.ts.
